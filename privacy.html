Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 November 2026 (visit counts and account deletion added)

REVEAL is a word guessing game. You can play every mode except ranked without an account. This page explains what we store and why.

If you play as a guest

  • Your settings, scores and records are kept only in your own browser (local storage).
  • If you post a Daily Challenge score, we store the nickname you type, your score, words solved, time taken and a random device id, so the leaderboard works. This is shown publicly on the leaderboard.
  • Online matches connect players' browsers directly (peer to peer). The name you type is shown to the other players in that match.

If you sign in with Google

  • Google shares your name, email address and profile picture link with us. We use your email only to identify your account. We don't show it to other players or send you marketing.
  • We store your player name, the avatar you pick, your game records (fastest answer, streak, correct answers, games, best score), your rating and your Daily Challenge scores.
  • Your player name, avatar, rating and posted scores are public to other players.

Where it's stored

Accounts and leaderboards are stored with Supabase, our database provider. The game itself is hosted on GitHub Pages. We use Google Fonts for typefaces.

Visit counts

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits and see which pages are used. It uses no cookies, doesn't track you across sites and doesn't identify you personally.

What we don't do

  • No ads and no ad tracking.
  • We don't sell or share your data with anyone else.

Deleting your data

You can sign out at any time. To delete your account and everything linked to it (player name, avatar, records, rating and Daily scores), open your account on the home screen and tap Delete my account. It's removed straight away. If you can't sign in any more, open a request on our support page with your player name, and we'll remove it within 30 days. Guest data can be cleared by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Children

REVEAL accounts are for players aged 13 and over.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

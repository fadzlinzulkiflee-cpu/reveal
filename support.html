Common questions

Do I need an account? No. Solo, Daily Challenge and Play Together work without one. You only need to sign in (Google, or Apple in the iPhone app) for Ranked Play.

How do I delete my account? Open your account on the home screen and tap Delete my account. Your player name, records, rating and Daily scores are removed straight away. If you can't sign in any more, email us with your player name and we'll remove it within 30 days.

Who are the BOT players? In online matches, empty seats are filled with bots so a game can start quickly. They're always marked BOT and never change your rating.

English or Bahasa? Use the English | Bahasa switch on the home screen to change the words and hints. Each language has its own Daily leaderboard.
